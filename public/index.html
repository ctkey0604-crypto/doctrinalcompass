What this is

A reference and self-assessment for Christian doctrine — built so you can explore and compare doctrine, not only test yourself. It states each position and renders no verdict on it.

How the result works

Each doctrine resolves to one position, never a blend. Your result is the tradition holding the most of those same positions outright. The percentage counts how many doctrines line up; the confidence score measures how firmly your answers point at that one tradition, weighing how exactly you matched it against how close the runner-up finished. Matching a tradition on every doctrine you settled identifies it outright, even where a neighbouring tradition finishes one doctrine behind. Answers stay in this browser and are never sent anywhere.

About the badges

The badges were drawn for this site. None is any church's official emblem and none is issued with anyone's permission or blessing. Holding one records what you answered, not what you belong to — churches receive people on their own terms, and no quiz can do it for them.
